Open your home. Change a life.

Foster homes are the heart of rescue. A spare room, a little patience, and a lot of love can give a cat or kitten the safe place they need while they wait for a forever family.

  • Give a kitty a calm, caring place to land
  • Help them grow confident and ready for adoption
  • Be part of their journey to a forever home

Kindness for community cats, too.

TNR stands for Trap-Neuter-Return: a humane approach that helps prevent future litters and supports healthier community cat colonies.

A kinder tomorrow

Helping community cats, one step at a time.

Community cats deserve compassion and care. Through TNR, cats are humanely trapped, spayed or neutered, and returned to their outdoor homes when appropriate.

Our heart, our mission

Because no paw should be left behind.

No Paws Left Behind Kitty Rescue is dedicated to helping homeless cats and kittens find safety, receive compassionate care, and move toward loving forever homes. We believe every life matters — tiny paws included.

Our work is powered by a community of fosters, adopters, volunteers, and supporters who believe in second chances.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How do I adopt a kitty?

Reach out through the contact section to ask about current adoptable cats and the rescue's adoption process. Application details will be linked here once confirmed.

Can I foster if I've never fostered before?

Absolutely ask us! We can talk through what fostering involves and whether it may be a good fit for your home.

What is TNR?

TNR means Trap-Neuter-Return, a humane community-cat approach that helps prevent future litters.

How else can I help?

Volunteer at events, help with transport, share rescue posts, foster, or make a donation through the rescue's confirmed official channels.
